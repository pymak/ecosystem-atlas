About this demo

Ecosystem Atlas is an interactive explorer for technology ecosystems. It shows where organizations are, what they work on, and how research institutions, labs, spin-offs and companies connect.

This portfolio edition uses a fictional dataset. Organization names, relationships and funding figures are invented. Positions are placed at real Swiss cities on a map drawn from official boundary data.

The Agent review view simulates how AI agents could keep the platform current: they propose new signals, events, relationships and profile updates with sources and a confidence score, and a person approves them before anything is published.

Edits and review decisions stay in this browser only. There are no accounts, external services or live data connections.
